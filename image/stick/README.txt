NUC Display Lab - USB survey stick

1. Plug this stick into the NUC and connect the NUC's HDMI port to the TV.
2. Turn the NUC on and press F10 for the boot menu. Choose the USB stick
   (the UEFI entry). If it will not boot, disable Secure Boot in the BIOS (F2).
3. Answer the three optional questions, then wait about a minute.
4. The report is uploaded automatically (wired network, or Wi-Fi - see
   wifi.txt) and a copy is saved in the reports folder on this stick.
5. Press P to power off, then remove the stick.

Nothing is installed and the NUC's internal drives are not touched.
The report contains hardware details, kernel logs and the TV's EDID
(model name and serial number), plus your answers. No personal files.
